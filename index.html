Your evaluation checklist

Six questions to ask any secure collaboration platform.

Federation

Does it speak the partner's protocol natively, or put a bridge or bot in the middle?

Native XMPP and Matrix. Real identities and attribution end to end, nothing in the middle.

Access control

Is access decided by attribute on every request, or by who holds membership or keys?

ABAC on clearance, program and citizenship, request by request, with an internal policy engine or your external PDP.

Accreditation scope

How many separate servers, plugins and services have to be accredited?

One platform and one security model for chat, voice, video, apps and AI.

The edge

Has it been proven on degraded links, or only in the lab?

Tested performant down to 9600 baud, built for the link to drop, integrated with TAK and ATAK.

AI

Where does the model run, and what can it see?

Any model inside your perimeter. No egress, and every result scoped to what the user may see.

Deployment

Is it the same build on every network, or re-engineered per environment?

A single codebase on-premises, in a secure cloud or fully air-gapped, across NIPR, SIPR and JWICS.
